What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a revolutionary field of artificial intelligence that produces entirely new forms of digital content including text, images, videos, code, music, and voice using deep learning.

It powers next-generation multimedia systems by transforming creative workflows, reducing production time, and enabling intelligent digital experiences.

How Does It Work?

GANs

Generative Adversarial Networks create realistic synthetic images and visuals.

Diffusion Models

Generate advanced visual outputs through progressive noise refinement.

Large Language Models

Systems like GPT generate human-like text, code, and conversation.

Neural Audio Models

Create realistic speech, music, and sound effects.
