What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is an advanced branch of artificial intelligence capable of creating text, images, videos, code, and sound by learning from vast datasets using deep neural networks.

It is revolutionizing multimedia systems, enabling unprecedented levels of creativity, automation, and digital innovation.

How Does It Work?

GANs

Create synthetic visuals using adversarial learning.

Diffusion Models

Generate realistic visual content through noise reconstruction.

Large Language Models

Systems like GPT generate advanced text and reasoning.

Voice Synthesis

AI audio systems produce realistic human speech.
